+Drive mods on your unit

For a unit running a build with the Drive Loader. This page reads the unit's build, lists its /mods folder, uploads .elemod files after checking them against that build, and chooses the set the unit loads at its next boot. It writes nothing outside /mods, and never flashes firmware.
